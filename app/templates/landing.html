About DiagnoPath AI

Building AI that is easier to understand.

DiagnoPath AI is an academic project focused on combining reinforcement-learning-based diagnostic actions with explainability techniques. Its goal is to make the reasoning behind system recommendations more transparent within a structured clinical workflow.

The project explores how AI can support the decision-making process while keeping clinical judgement and professional responsibility with the healthcare professional.

✓
Explainability by design Inspect model explanations instead of treating suggestions as unquestionable answers.
✓
Human-centred decisions Keep clinicians responsible for evaluating findings and making final decisions.
✓
Responsible communication Present AI outputs as decision support, not as a replacement for medical expertise.
